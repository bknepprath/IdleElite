Screen Intent

The hub should open on a weak, unfinished farm lot. The signs make the hotspots readable before the buildings exist, while the dirt patches make upgrades feel visually earned.

The first shippable build can make the Fish Pond real and leave Barn, Mission Sign, and Garden as clear labeled shells, with the Trophy Platform as a passive display spot.
