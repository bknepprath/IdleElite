# Activity Database Contract

## Page swipe preparation

Neighbor preparation shares the current page's mount budget without treating its own previous mount as a new blocker. It starts after one free frame, prioritizes the requested direction, finishes that side before the opposite side, and performs bounded preparation during horizontal drags and settle animations. A reversal retires an unfinished unseen opposite page only at a render yield boundary. Twelve cheap lazy placeholders may be created per frame; real card mounting remains separately bounded. A drag completes only the incoming side synchronously, and promotion keeps the same mounted page and scroll state. Tree arena controls explicitly lay out their button faces and labels during setup, refresh and resize so disabled neighbor pages do not arrive with stacked controls. Validate with `scripts/tests/swipe_latency_audit.gd` and the existing adjacent-page, first-swipe, fresh-save and no-snapback regressions. Evidence: `playtests/2026-10-26-swipe-latency.md`.

## Shared collection feedback

Live inventory rewards publish `MaterialRuntime.item_collected` receipts containing the action key, item ID, actual credited amount and per-item harvest count. Standard material grants use `collect_amount`; separately stored seasonal balances use `report_collected_item` after crediting their actual delta. The material collection surface batches those receipts by action and routes each positive item to its matching visible holder. No activity-name check or inferred reward belongs in the animation dispatch. Item metadata and wallet storage remain independent of this rule.

`has_item_boxes` and `item_box_ids` determine ordinary gathering holders from reward definitions, including registered seasonal items. All fifteen current standard-holder activities use the same receipt-to-pop/drop/pulse path. Failed rolls, zero credit, capped balances, inventory restoration, spending and offline grants must not replay collection effects. `add_amount` remains available for balance operations and the existing dedicated factory/arena reward sequences. Factories retain their recipe conversion and output-delivery event; groves retain their per-log harvest animation.

Flyers follow a curved arc from the actual action art to the current target holder, including layout motion, then fade after arrival. The holder pulse begins at arrival. Flyer rotation/scale use a fixed 180px canvas; animations never grant inventory. Flyers join the shared reward-effect cleanup group, and disappear if their holder is destroyed or hidden. Berry double-harvest burst counts belong to individual receipts, so they cannot duplicate another item in a mixed reward.

Validate with `scripts/tests/collection_rewards.gd`, `production_paths.gd`, the actual-main `collection_rewards_capture.gd` and factory inventory capture. See `playtests/2026-10-26-shared-collection-feedback.md`.

## Required Change Flow

`unlock_drop` defines dedicated progression resources (`id`, `name`, integer `amount`, `icon`) produced on successful completion. These are not inventory currencies. Opening, resting, passive, Wisdom-only and arena activities do not receive these drops. Amounts currently increase with source level and remain prototype balance.

`unlock_project` version 2 retains stable project and step IDs and integer targets, but steps name a `resource` rather than requiring one specific activity. `source_skill`/`source_action` remain provenance and compatibility metadata. The runtime routes each unit to the earliest unfinished revealed compatible blocker (owner activity level order), carrying surplus forward without parallel duplication. Blocker clicks choose the highest current expected production rate among unlocked sources, using actual cycle time and success chance; equal rates favor higher levels. Resource icons are shared by activity families rather than assigned one per skill. `trail-marks` is the retained save/data ID for Resin; Rivets and Coins are additional resources. `unlock_project.material` selects timber, stone, iron, bronze, silver, gold, titanium, platinum or obsidian. These materials use shared ImageGen cover frames, holders and debris sprites. Portrait badges and blocker faces show icons and curved progress without numeric yields or fractions.

`activity_projects` save version 2 retains permanent counters. Version 1 success counters become equivalent resource credit without loss; old `materials` flags remain serialized for monotonic cloud comparisons. Project inventory costs have been removed. Do not rename project or step IDs without migration. Work/resources/Wisdom clear the illustrated barrier, then normal level and other access rules still apply. Old legitimate access is grandfathered. A requirement for a skill-wide resource (a skill's `skill_unlock_drop`) asks for that entry's `target_multiplier` times the listed amount; `activity_projects.target_multipliers` records the multipliers a save's amounts are counted against, and loading scales amounts saved under a lower one so that finished work stays finished. Run `scripts/tests/progression_prototype.gd` through the safe wrapper after progression changes; it delegates to the current production-scene resource test.

`mastery_xp_per_completion` is optional and defaults to 1. Resting activities use 10 to reduce completion requirements to approximately one tenth without changing saved mastery XP or levels. Arena activities (`combat.arena_shape = "diamond"`) do not earn mastery or count toward medal requirements.

Activity success uses each explicit action `success` value plus existing additive equipment, medal, tier, Barn and trophy effects, clamped at 100%. There is no global diminishing-returns curve above 90%. Recovery, passive and convergence activities retain their existing guarantees, as does the starter completion guarantee. Tune individual base rates in this JSON; do not compress earned bonuses. Verify with `scripts/test-ham-oct21.ps1`.
