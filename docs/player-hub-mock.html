Screen Intent

The hub should open on a weak, unfinished farm lot. The signs make the hotspots readable before the buildings exist, while the dirt patches make upgrades feel visually earned.

The first shippable build can make the Fish Pond real and leave Barn, Trophy Case, Training Ring, and Notice Board as clear labeled shells.
