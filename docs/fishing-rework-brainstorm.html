Current Behavior

  • Fishing renders through the Godot rework path in _render_fishing_area_modules.
  • Region modules come from fishing.areas[]; rewards and unlocks come from fishing.actions[].
  • Beach and Pier have authored location tiles in FISHING_LOCATION_DEFS. Later regions currently use their area action rows.
  • The fish wallet and circular tool menu are live. Equipped tool state is saved as equipped_fishing_tool_id.
  • Net and boat use stored catch thresholds; rods and mirror adjust timing, XP, and catch behavior.
