Idle Elite Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Idle Elite is provided by WeSkillNow. Contact: weskillnow@gmail.com.

Game and account data

The game stores progress, settings and saves on your device, including browser storage when you play on the web. Online features use an anonymous Firebase account identifier. You may link a Google account for cloud backup and recovery. Firebase Authentication processes your account identifier and Google account information, including email, when you link Google.

Firebase Realtime Database stores your public player name, selected avatar, leaderboard scores and timestamps, public chat messages, and moderation records. Google-linked cloud saves contain gameplay progress and settings and may include previous save revisions for recovery. Chat reports contain the reported message identifier, reporter identifier, reason and time. Your blocked-player list is stored in your save.

Account data enables sign-in and recovery. Gameplay data enables progress backup and rankings. Chat data enables public conversation, reports and moderation. We use authentication and rate limits to prevent abuse. Other players can see your public name, avatar, rankings and posted chat messages; cloud saves and reports are not publicly readable.

Advertising

Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob on Android and Google AdSense H5 Games Ads in the browser where available. Google may process device and advertising identifiers, cookies and similar browser storage, IP addresses and approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to provide and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Google’s consent service provides privacy choices where required. On Android, revisit available choices in Settings → Privacy & chat → Ad privacy choices. When a browser consent message is displayed, use its Consent, Do not consent, or Manage options controls. You can revisit browser consent through the Privacy and cookie settings link provided with that message. Declining consent does not prevent normal gameplay; ad availability and personalization may differ.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

Support and diagnostics

If you contact support, we receive what you send, including your email and any attached files. Crash reports are stored locally; the game offers a control to copy a report for you to share. We do not automatically receive those local crash files.

Sharing and security

Firebase, Google AdMob and Google AdSense process data for the features described above. Public profile, leaderboard and chat information is shared with other players. Network requests use HTTPS. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset or remove it. Online account, cloud-save, profile, leaderboard and chat records remain until removed through account deletion or moderation. Uninstalling or using Hard Reset does not delete online records.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data, or specific online data, through Settings → Privacy & chat → Delete account / data or the account deletion page. We verify ownership before deleting data. The deletion covers Firebase Authentication, current and previous cloud saves, profile and name claims, leaderboard records, chat messages and associated reports. Information we are legally required to retain is kept only for that requirement and is explained when responding to the request.

Changes

We update this policy when data practices change and revise the effective date.
