Idle Elite Chat Rules

Version 1. Effective October 23, 2026.

By agreeing in the game before posting, you accept these chat rules.

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content or content exploiting children.
  • No sharing someone’s private information, scams, impersonation, spam or illegal content.
  • Messages are public. Do not post passwords, contact details or other private information.

Use the message’s menu to report a violation or block its author. Blocked players’ messages are hidden on your device. Manage blocks in Settings → Privacy & chat → Blocked players. Reports go to the developer for review. Violating content may be removed and chat access restricted.

Contact weskillnow@gmail.com for moderation questions or appeals. Privacy policy.
